Lesson 7 — CSS Grid

Grid lays out in two dimensions: rows and columns at once. Use it for page layouts, galleries and dashboards. Flex and grid combine freely.

Defining tracks

  • fr is a fraction of the leftover space. repeat(3, 1fr) makes three equal columns.
  • minmax(200px, 1fr): a track at least 200px, at most one share.
  • Lines are numbered from 1; negative numbers count from the end (-1 is the last line).

The responsive grid with no media queries

Resize the preview. Columns appear and disappear automatically.

auto-fill keeps empty tracks; auto-fit collapses them so items stretch to fill the row. The min(140px, 100%) keeps it from overflowing very narrow screens.

Named areas

Alignment

  • Items inside cells: justify-items, align-items, shorthand place-items: center.
  • The whole track set inside the container: justify-content, align-content.
  • One item: justify-self, align-self, place-self.
  • Implicit tracks: grid-auto-rows: minmax(100px, auto); grid-auto-flow: dense fills gaps (mind reading order).

Subgrid and overlap

Flex or grid?Content-driven and one axis (navbars, button groups) → flex. Layout-driven and two axes (pages, galleries, dashboards) → grid.

Checkpoint

fr vs %?% is a share of the container's full size and ignores gaps. fr divides the space left after gaps and fixed tracks.
auto-fit vs auto-fill?Both create as many tracks as fit. auto-fill keeps empty ones; auto-fit collapses them so existing items grow.
